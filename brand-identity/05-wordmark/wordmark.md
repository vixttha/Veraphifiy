# Wordmark

Use **Veraphifiy** as the draft wordmark lettering, following the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json). Retain the initial capital V and the exact ending `-fiy`.

| Treatment | Three ways it supports the identity | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Index Sans** | Square terminals echo the symbol. Open counters preserve small-size clarity. Even word weights give equal emphasis to the graph and the record. | Subtle enough that the mark carries much of the character. |
| **Split Weight** | A medium “Ver” suggests verification. Semibold “aphifiy” emphasizes the Graphify echo. The weight change shows where the two parts of the wordplay meet. | Too much weight contrast can make one word read like two labels. |
| **Archive Serif** | Sturdy serifs reference index tabs. Book-like proportions fit a permanent record. The serif texture distinguishes it from interface text. | Fine details need a larger minimum size. |

Lead with **Index Sans** in Instrument Sans semibold. Start with natural tracking and check that the final “fiy” stays readable at the smallest placement. Align the cap height optically to the mark's center. Use a provisional 24 px mark with 8 px between mark and lettering. Keep one cap-height of clear space around the full lockup. Check at 120 px wide and fall back to live text if the letterforms lose clarity.

Use the symbol alone where the product name is already provided, wordmark alone where width is limited, and the combined lockup in the primary README header. Supply accessible name text once when the website or GitHub placement permits.
