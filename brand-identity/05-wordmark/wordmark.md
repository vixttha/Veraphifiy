# Wordmark

Use the exact proposed lettering **Edge Docket**, following the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json). Retain the space and title case in text and artwork.

| Treatment | Three ways it supports the identity | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Index Sans** | Square terminals echo the symbol. Open counters preserve small-size clarity. Even word weights give equal emphasis to the graph and the record. | Subtle enough that the mark carries much of the character. |
| **Split Weight** | A medium “Edge” suggests the path. A semibold “Docket” anchors the record. The weight change gives a scanning cue in wide placements. | The emphasis may read as a two-part product name. |
| **Archive Serif** | Sturdy serifs reference index tabs. Book-like proportions fit a permanent record. The serif texture distinguishes it from interface text. | Fine details need a larger minimum size. |

Lead with **Index Sans** in Instrument Sans semibold. Start with natural tracking and adjust spacing optically around “ge” and “Do” after drawing. Align the cap height optically to the mark's center. Use a provisional 24 px mark with 8 px between mark and lettering. Keep one cap-height of clear space around the full lockup. Check at 120 px wide and fall back to live text if the letterforms lose clarity.

Use the symbol alone where the product name is already provided, wordmark alone where width is limited, and the combined lockup in the primary README header. Supply accessible name text once when the website or GitHub placement permits.
