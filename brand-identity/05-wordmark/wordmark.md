# Wordmark

Use **GEvidence** as the draft wordmark lettering, following the [creative decision](../01-strategy/creative-decision.json). Retain the capital G and E and keep the lettering together.

| Treatment | Three ways it supports the identity | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Index Sans** | Square terminals echo the symbol. Open counters preserve small-size clarity. Even word weights give equal emphasis to the graph and the record. | Subtle enough that the mark carries much of the character. |
| **Split Weight** | A medium “G” suggests the graph. Semibold “Evidence” gives the supporting record emphasis. The weight change reveals how the two ideas combine in one name. | Too much weight contrast can make the name look like two labels. |
| **Archive Serif** | Sturdy serifs reference index tabs. Book-like proportions fit a permanent record. The serif texture distinguishes it from interface text. | Fine details need a larger minimum size. |

Lead with **Index Sans** in Instrument Sans semibold. Start with natural tracking and inspect the “GE” and “Ev” pairs at the smallest placement. Align the cap height optically to the mark's center. Use a provisional 24 px mark with 8 px between mark and lettering. Keep one cap-height of clear space around the full lockup. Check at 120 px wide and fall back to live text if the letterforms lose clarity.

Use the symbol alone where the product name is already provided, wordmark alone where width is limited, and the combined lockup in the primary README header. Supply accessible name text once when the website or GitHub placement permits.
