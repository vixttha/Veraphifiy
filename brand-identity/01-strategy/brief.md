# Strategy and creative brief

- Product category and one concrete thing the product does.
- Primary audience, the moment they discover it, and the decision they need to make.
- First surface and its smallest meaningful display size.
- Desired qualities with visible effects. Translate each adjective into a decision about shape, type, color, or spacing.
- Useful references and nearby brands to distinguish. For each reference, record its source and the specific quality being studied.
- Owner input, agent proposal, and open decisions.

For local evidence stored elsewhere in the adopting repository, use a path relative to this brief. For example, if the repository has `docs/evidence.md` at its root, use `../../docs/evidence.md` as the link target. Local links must point to an existing target inside the repository after resolving symlinks.

Start with the [naming record](../02-naming/naming.md) and [visual system](../06-visual-system/system.md).
