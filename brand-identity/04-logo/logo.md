# Logo symbol

**Direction.** A flat geometric mark anchored in repository evidence. Scorecards use the [template weights](../01-strategy/creative-direction.md). Scores reflect concept review before drawing.

| Concept | What to draw | Three meanings | Trade-off | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Filed Edge** | A low source bar, one rising diagonal, and a tall tab with a shallow notch | The bar is the cited line. The diagonal is a graph edge. The tab files the evidence for later review. | Keep the notch wide enough to read at 24 px. | **95/100** |
| **Source Bracket** | Two offset brackets joined by a stepped path | The brackets delimit source locations. The path follows a relationship. The vertical step shows movement from a feature claim to evidence. | Brackets are common in developer marks. | 85/100 |
| **Paired Receipt** | Two offset sheets with aligned top corners along a diagonal | One sheet is the graph. One is the receipt. Their alignment represents the shared source. | The silhouette needs room to show both sheets. | 77/100 |

Use Filed Edge as the lead concept for review. Start with a 24-unit square, a centered silhouette, consistent stroke thickness and at least one stroke of clear space on all sides. Evaluate both the outer silhouette and the tab cutout at 24 px. Test a single ink color on light and dark surfaces before introducing copper. The standalone mark fits the avatar or favicon, subject to size testing.

Keep the mark independent from upstream branding. Favor squared geometry and one meaningful connection over a many-node network. Use the [wordmark](../05-wordmark/wordmark.md) for lockup sizing and [review](../08-review/review.md) for export checks.
